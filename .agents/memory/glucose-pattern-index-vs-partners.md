---
name: Glucose pattern index vs partners
description: Product rules separating ranked HStix components from explanatory meal partners.
---

Only carb and sweet components can become General/HStix index entries or appear under “More values expected.” Ordinary authoritative foods may still explain a ranked component as meal partners, but must never become index entries themselves.

Directional HStix results are selected on the backend: Higher keeps the five largest lift ratios and Lower keeps the five smallest, with stable food-key tie-breaking. No-significant-difference keeps the existing five-food client sampling behavior.

**Why:** Index cards are actionable glucose-pattern claims and need the stricter carb/sweet eligibility rule. Ordinary foods still carry useful combination context, so excluding them from partner analysis would hide real explanations.

**How to apply:** Keep index eligibility and partner eligibility as separate gates. Preserve the existing High/total result sentence on ranked cards without exposing average mmol/L or lift.
