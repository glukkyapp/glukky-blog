Corner radius
Rounded corners strongly shape the app’s personality, and larger radii generally feel softer and friendlier. A large mobile card radius around 24–32px is consistent with modern expressive design systems.

Main cards: 28px

Smaller pills / icon circles: 20px

Buttons: 22px

Bottom nav container: 28px

Input / small tag: 16px

If you nest cards or panels, the inner radius should usually equal outer radius minus padding so the curves align cleanly.
