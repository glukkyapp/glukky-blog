Rename the current Weekly Report to Monthly Report and replace the current weekly insights with three monthly meal-pattern comparisons. Remove the incomplete-record card and stop generating advice from a single week’s weekday result.

Monthly report changes
1. Rename and time window
Rename all user-facing references from Weekly Report / 本週 to Monthly Report / 上個月.

Use the previous completed calendar month as the reporting window.

A meal can be included whether its impact label comes from AI or HStix.

Every meal must contribute only one impact label:

Use the recorded HStix result when an eligible HStix reading exists.

Otherwise use the existing AI meal-impact label.

Do not double-count a meal when both AI and HStix data exist.

2. Use one shared calculation
For every eligible bucket:

Higher-impact rate
=
number of higher-impact meals in the bucket
total logged meals in the bucket
Higher-impact rate= 
total logged meals in the bucket
number of higher-impact meals in the bucket
​
 
Use the rate, never only the raw number of higher-impact meals.

A meal may contribute to more than one comparison:

A Monday lunch contributes to the Monday bucket.

The same meal contributes to the lunch bucket.

The same meal contributes to the weekday bucket.

This is expected because each card answers a different question.

3. Add three comparison cards
Monthly card	Buckets compared	Eligibility rule
Highest-impact mealtime	Breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack	Each included mealtime requires at least 8 logged meals. At least two mealtimes must qualify.
Highest-impact weekday	Monday through Sunday	Each included weekday requires at least 3 logged meals. At least two weekdays must qualify.
Weekday vs weekend	Monday–Friday vs Saturday–Sunday	Weekdays require at least 10 logged meals; weekends require at least 4 logged meals. Both must qualify.
For mealtime, do not require snack to qualify. If breakfast, lunch, and dinner each have 8+ meals but snack has only 2, compare breakfast/lunch/dinner and exclude snack.

For weekday, do not require all seven weekdays to qualify. Compare only the weekdays with at least three logged meals. If fewer than two qualify, do not show a highest-weekday result.

4. Ranking and no-pattern states
Rank qualified buckets by higher-impact rate.

If two buckets have the same rate:

Prefer the bucket with more logged meals.

If still tied, use a fixed order so the result never changes randomly:

Mealtime: breakfast → lunch → dinner → snack.

Weekday: Monday → Tuesday → Wednesday → Thursday → Friday → Saturday → Sunday.

Weekday/weekend: weekday → weekend.

If a card has insufficient eligible buckets, show no comparison card rather than a weak conclusion.

If qualified buckets do not show a meaningful difference under the existing product rule, show a neutral “no clear difference this month” state rather than forcing a worst meal/day.

5. Elderly-readable copy
Do not write “best diet,” “worst diet,” or “you must pay more attention.” Use observed, time-bounded wording.

Suggested Chinese copy:

Weekday vs weekend

Avoid adding a food recommendation to these cards unless it is supported by the separate food-frequency or HStix food-pattern feature. A day-of-week or mealtime observation alone does not establish that a specific food caused the difference.

6. Remove or relocate old cards
Remove 「1日的飲食記錄不完整」 from the report.

Do not show a card saying one weekday was “best” and another must be watched based on one week.

Do not display separate lunch-timing and lunch-impact cards as if they explain one another. The monthly mealtime card replaces the glucose-impact part; timing can remain a separate descriptive feature only if it has its own evidence and does not imply causation.
