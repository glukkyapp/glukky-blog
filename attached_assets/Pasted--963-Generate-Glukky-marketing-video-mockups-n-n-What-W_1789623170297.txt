#963 - Generate Glukky marketing video mockups\n\n## What & Why\nCreate two short promotional video mockups for Glukky marketing and App Store use. The clips must show real current app flows, use the existing navigation and color palette, and make the featured interactions visually clear without inventing product behavior or changing production copy.\n\n## Done looks like\n- Two separate short video mockups are delivered: one for the Homepage old-photo reward flow and one for Foodsnap leading to Glucose Pattern insights.\n- Both clips include the app navigation bar exactly as it currently appears and retain the current Glukky color palette.\n- The Homepage clip shows the real daily health task action, the real completion condition, and the resulting old-photo unlock/reveal using the existing flow and an enhanced but faithful presentation.\n- The Foodsnap clip shows a real or clearly supported meal example, transitions to Glucose Pattern, and uses the exact current localized strings for the higher-than-usual and food-pairing insights.\n- The final handoff identifies the specific screens, components, and functions referenced for each clip.\n- Any requested behavior that does not exist in the current build is explicitly flagged instead of being fabricated.\n\n## Out of scope\n- Changing production app behavior, navigation, copy, analytics, or reward logic.\n- Introducing a new visual theme or colors that are not already in the app.\n- Inventing a daily-task completion trigger, old-photo reward behavior, glucose insight type, or food-pairing result.\n- Adding a new native app artifact or altering the existing web app.\n\n## Steps\n1. Audit the real flows -- Trace the Homepage daily health task and old-photo reward from the current code, including the exact completion trigger, data state, reveal transition, and navigation behavior. Record any mismatch between the brief and the implementation before designing the clip.\n2. Audit the Foodsnap insight flow -- Trace meal capture/logging into Glucose Pattern and identify the exact current strings and data conditions for the higher-than-usual and pairing insights. Resolve the example meal from supported app data or flag it if the requested scenario cannot be reproduced.\n3. Build the Homepage clip -- Create a short mockup that starts from the real Homepage state, performs the real task action, and presents the real photo reward with a stronger but faithful reveal treatment.\n4. Build the Foodsnap clip -- Create a separate short mockup that shows the supported Foodsnap meal flow, transitions to Glucose Pattern, and emphasizes the actual insight cards/callouts without changing their underlying logic or wording.\n5. Verify and document -- Confirm both clips preserve the current navigation and palette, do not fabricate unavailable behavior, and document the exact source screens/components/functions used for each deliverable.\n\n## Relevant files\n- client/src/pages/home.tsx\n- client/src/components/piggy-bank-card.tsx\n- client/src/components/piggy-bank-svg.tsx\n- client/src/pages/snap.tsx\n- client/src/pages/glucose-patterns.tsx\n- client/src/components/floating-nav-bar.tsx\n- client/src/lib/glucose-pattern-ranking.ts\n- client/src/index.css\n- client/src/locales/en.json\n- client/src/locales/zh-Hant.json\n- client/src/locales/yue.json\n
