CLIP 2: Foodsnap -> Glucose Pattern Insights
====================================================================

CONFIRMED REAL BEHAVIOR:
- Foodsnap flow: user picks Camera or Album (native file input) -> selects meal type (breakfast/lunch/dinner/snack) -> taps Continue (snap.tsx:940-1036).
- Glucose Pattern card per food shows: food name, an ImpactBadge (High/Medium/Low), a description string, and a reading count "X high readings from Y meals" (glucose-patterns.tsx:445-468).
- Real impact copy (en.json:1312-1319):
  - High: "Higher impact compared with your other recorded meals"
  - Medium: "Similar impact compared with your other recorded meals"
  - Low: "Lower impact compared with your other recorded meals"
- Real food-pairing copy (glucose-patterns.tsx:469-496):
  - Dominant partner: "Most times you eat {{indexFood}}, you also eat {{partner}}. The reading may reflect both foods together."
  - Comparison: "After eating {{indexFood}}, your average reading was higher with {{higherPartner}} and lower with {{lowerPartner}}."
  - Disclaimer (always shown): "This is a pattern in your records. It does not prove that either food caused the difference. Portion size, other foods, activity, and timing can also affect readings."

WHY THE ORIGINAL BRIEF WAS WRONG:
- "Glucose higher than usual after this food" is NOT the real string. The real string is "Higher impact compared with your other recorded meals."
- "Pairing it with ___ gives a larger glucose impact" is more causal/definitive than the real product language, which is carefully hedged ("average reading was higher with...") and always paired with an explicit non-causal disclaimer. Using the stronger phrasing would misrepresent the app AND reintroduce the exact kind of overclaiming risk flagged in the App Store description review (avoid treatment/causal-outcome claims).

CORRECTED SHOT SEQUENCE:
1. Open on Foodsnap, nav bar visible.
2. User taps Camera or Album, selects a real supported food photo.
3. User selects a meal type (e.g., Dinner) and taps Continue.
4. Transition to Glucose Pattern page via nav bar (glucose/TrendingUp icon).
5. Show the logged food's card: food name, "Higher impact" badge, and the real description "Higher impact compared with your other recorded meals," plus the reading count line.
6. Show the food-pairing insight using the REAL comparison copy: "After eating {{indexFood}}, your average reading was higher with {{higherPartner}} and lower with {{lowerPartner}}."
7. Include the disclaimer text on screen (even briefly) rather than omitting it -- it's part of the real product experience and should not be cut for the sake of a cleaner-looking clip.
