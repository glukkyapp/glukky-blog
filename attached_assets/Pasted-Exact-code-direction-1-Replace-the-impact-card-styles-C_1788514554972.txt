Keep colour only in status UI
Keep the impact classification visible through small, contained elements rather than the entire card.

For example:

tsx
const IMPACT_BADGE_STYLES = {
  low: "border-[#55B98A] bg-[#F2FBF6] text-[#26734F]",

Then apply it only to the status pill:

tsx
<span
  className={cn(
    "rounded-full border px-3 py-1 text-sm font-semibold",

  {impactLabel}

The same principle applies to your existing three filter controls: preserve their green/yellow/red styling, but do not let those semantic colors take over the full information card.

Important safeguard
Do not change this wrapper:

tsx
<div
  className="
    touch-pan-y
    overflow-hidden
    rounded-xl
    outline-none
    focus-visible:ring-2
    focus-visible:ring-ring
    focus-visible:ring-offset-2
  "
  data-testid="pattern-card-viewport"
>
It already matches the Profile corner radius and controls clipping during the swipe transition. Changing it risks creating clipped corners or visible background gaps during card movement.
