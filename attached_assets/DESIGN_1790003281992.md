## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for dual-generation health stewardship in Hong Kong: elderly individuals self-monitoring metabolic data and adult children/caregivers overseeing longitudinal care plans. The interface prioritizes high-contrast legibility, immediate semantic comprehension, and editorial medical dignity. 

Rather than chasing modern consumer-tech visual tropes, the visual language establishes an atmosphere akin to a trusted private clinic or a meticulously printed medical journal. The aesthetic reconciles clinical authority with familial warmth through tactile paper tones, resolute deep teal inks, and grounded structural stability.

### Key Tenets
- **Geriatric Accessibility First**: Absolute prioritization of legibility, touch affordance, and clear reading paths over compact information density. Generous proportions prevent motor error and optical fatigue.
- **Bi-Lingual Harmony**: Seamless typesetting balancing Hong Kong Traditional Chinese typography (Noto Sans TC / PingFang TC) alongside English metrics, drug names, and clinical readings.
- **Zero Decorative Noise (Anti-AI)**: Strictly eliminate decorative gradient meshes, glowing neon rings, floating glass cards, and speculative "AI copilot" visual artifacts. AI-assisted insights must appear as rigorous, plain-text clinician review cards with explicit clinical provenance and review timestamps.
- **Semantic Truth**: Colors are reserved strictly for behavioral and metabolic state verification. No element receives hue without operational purpose.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unshakeable sense of calm and clarity. It replaces blinding digital white with soothing, low-strain paper-inspired creams, while replacing harsh pure black (#000000) with a deep, mineral-rich Brand Ink (#173F46) engineered to maximize visual acuity for aging retinas and cataract-affected eyes.

### Palette Application Guidelines

- **Surface Base**:
  - `page-bg`: `#FCFBF2` (Warm linen cream). The universal background canvas, reducing eye fatigue during regular morning and evening glucose logging.
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white) for primary reading surfaces, paired with `#FFF8EC` (Warm cream tinted surface) for contextual notices, medication logs, and secondary grouping containers.
  - `surface-subtle`: `#F4F2E4` for inactive states, table headers, and divider backgrounds.

- **Brand & Action Tier**:
  - `primary`: `#0D7E8F` (Clinical Teal). Used exclusively for primary interactive controls, main navigation active states, and validated health actions.
  - `primary-dark`: `#086574`. Mandatory for pressed/hover button states and critical text links on light containers to surpass 7:1 contrast ratios.
  - `primary-soft`: `#D7EEF0`. Pill chip backgrounds, active tab selections, and focused record backgrounds.
  - `primary-wash`: `#E8F5F3`. Full-width alert backdrops and clinical banner fills.

- **Typography & Structural Inks**:
  - `text-primary`: `#173F46` (Deep Sea Ink). Delivers stark contrast against cream surfaces while avoiding the sterile severity of jet black.
  - `text-muted`: `#5C7477` (Muted Slate). Restricted strictly to secondary labels, units of measure (e.g., `mmol/L`), and timestamps. Must never be rendered below 14px.
  - `hairline-border`: `#E2E0D2`. Crisp structural divider lines, input field borders, and segmented card edges.

- **Semantic Metabolic Indicators (Strictly Functional)**:
  - *Low Glucose (Hypoglycemia)*:
    - Text/Border: `#166534` (Deep Emerald)
    - Surface Wash: `#ECFDF5`
    - Indicator Pip: `#22C55E`
  - *Target Glucose (Normal Range)*:
    - Text/Border: `#086574` (Deep Teal)
    - Surface Wash: `#E8F5F3`
    - Indicator Pip: `#0D7E8F`
  - *Elevated Glucose (Pre-Prandial Warning)*:
    - Text/Border: `#92400E` (Deep Amber)
    - Surface Wash: `#FFFBEB`
    - Indicator Pip: `#F59E0B`
  - *High Glucose (Hyperglycemia / Critical Alert)*:
    - Text/Border: `#991B1B` (Deep Coral Red)
    - Surface Wash: `#FEF2F2`
    - Indicator Pip: `#EF4444`

## Typography

The type scale is built around radical accessibility and bi-lingual readability. Standard web body sizes (14px-16px) are intentionally prohibited for primary content. Body type defaults to 18px and scales to 20px for key instructions. 

### Font Fallbacks & Localization
- **System Stack**: `"Noto Sans TC", "PingFang TC", "Microsoft JhengHei", "Noto Sans", system-ui, sans-serif`.
- Numeric values utilize tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) so that sequential blood glucose logs, insulin dosage tallies, and timestamps align across rows without horizontal optical jitter.

### Typography Rules
1. **Never Shrink for Space**: If text overflows its container, stack vertically or wrap to next line; never truncate vital medical dosage instructions or warning labels with ellipses (`...`).
2. **Generous Line Spacing**: Traditional Chinese characters require vertical breathing room due to internal stroke density. All Traditional Chinese body text adheres strictly to a 1.65 to 1.7 line-height ratio.
3. **Restricted Weights**: Use Regular (400) for narrative reading and SemiBold/Bold (600/700) for structural signposts and glucose metrics. Ultra-light, thin, or hairline weights are banned across all viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an uncluttered single-column or dual-column structural card format, avoiding complex multi-nested split panes that disorient older adults.

### Layout Principles
- **Touch Bounds**: Every clickable, tappable, or selectable element must provide a minimum physical interaction footprint of **48×48px** (ideally 56px for primary buttons and bottom navigation anchors).
- **Mobile Container (Elder Portability)**: 1-column layout with 20px (`1.25rem`) side margins. Forms and cards stretch across the full content column to maximize thumb-press targets.
- **Tablet / Desktop (Caregiver Dashboard)**: A centered, constrained container capped at **1080px** width. Patient timeline on the left (7 columns), clinician notes and action checklists on the right (5 columns).
- **Rhythm**: Spacing follows an 8px base grid. Structural component separations use `space-lg` (32px) and `space-xl` (48px) to provide clear spatial grouping between distinct health metrics (e.g., fasting vs. post-prandial records).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids optical gimmickry, skeuomorphic glare, and disorienting multi-layered depth models. Diffuse drop shadows and bright ambient glows are replaced with structured tonal layering and architectural hairline boundaries.

### Depth Architecture
1. **Flat Base**: The canvas remains permanently seated at `#FCFBF2`.
2. **Structural Enclosures (Elevation Level 0)**: Content grouping relies on crisp 1px solid borders (`#E2E0D2`) set against either pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale cream (`#FFF8EC`) backgrounds.
3. **Raised Interactive Cards (Elevation Level 1)**: For cards requiring tangible physical elevation (such as a tap-to-expand glucose reading or pending insulin verification card), use an anchored, low-blur tactile shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 2px 4px rgba(23, 63, 70, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(23, 63, 70, 0.06);`
   - Border remains active: `1px solid #E2E0D2`.
4. **Modals & Emergency Confirmations (Elevation Level 2)**:
   - Backdrop: Warm opaque veil (`rgba(23, 63, 70, 0.45)`) that dims distracting background noise.
   - Container: `#FFFFFF` surfaced card with a grounded drop shadow: `0 12px 28px rgba(23, 63, 70, 0.12)`.
   - Never use frosted glass (`backdrop-filter: blur()`) behind critical alerts, as it reduces contrast and visual clarity for impaired sight.

## Shapes

Corner geometry strikes a deliberate balance between clinical structure and approachable warmth. Elements employ medium rounding (`roundedness: 2`, 8px base / 16px for cards), ensuring cards feel friendly and human without turning into novelty bubbles.

### Shape Scale Rules
- **Cards & Data Panels**: 16px (`1rem`) corner radius. Accented with hairline borders.
- **Form Controls & Inputs**: 10px corner radius. Solid, visible tactile containers that clearly look like fillable receptacles.
- **Buttons**: 10px corner radius for standard functional buttons. Pill shapes (9999px) are strictly limited to small category chips and status indicators to avoid competing with main action buttons.
- **Pills / Status Badges**: Fully rounded ends (9999px) to clearly indicate non-clickable status or category classifications.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action Button**:
  - Height: Minimum 54px.
  - Background: `#0D7E8F` (Hover/Active: `#086574`).
  - Text: `#FFFFFF`, 18px SemiBold.
  - Border: None.
  - Focus Ring: 3px solid `#D7EEF0` with a 2px offset.
- **Secondary Action Button**:
  - Height: Minimum 54px.
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`.
  - Border: 2px solid `#0D7E8F`.
  - Text: `#0D7E8F`, 18px SemiBold.
- **Destructive / High Alert Button**:
  - Background: `#FEF2F2`.
  - Border: 2px solid `#991B1B`.
  - Text: `#991B1B`, 18px Bold.

### Blood Glucose Status Cards
- The heart of the daily routine. Composed of a pure white canvas framed with `1px solid #E2E0D2`.
- Left-hand anchor: A solid 6px vertical indicator bar colored strictly in the relevant semantic indicator (`#166534`, `#0D7E8F`, `#92400E`, or `#991B1B`).
- Value Display: Numerical reading rendered in `metric-display` (40px/48px tabular bold), immediately followed by the label `mmol/L` in 18px `#5C7477`.
- Context Badge: A pill badge stating the context (e.g., "空腹 Fasting" or "餐後 2小時 2h Post-Meal") set in 16px Medium with a neutral background (`#F4F2E4`) and `#173F46` text.

### Form Inputs & Steppers
- **Text & Number Inputs**:
  - Container: White background with a 1.5px solid `#E2E0D2` border. Height: 56px.
  - Typography: 20px Regular `#173F46`.
  - Placeholder: `#5C7477` at 18px.
  - Focused state: 2px solid `#0D7E8F` with no glowing shadow.
- **Quick-Adjust Steppers (+ / -)**:
  - Designed for elderly users entering numerical values without invoking software keyboards.
  - Large 56×56px square touch triggers on either side of the number field with thick 24px icon glyphs.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Height: 44px (large enough for steady finger activation).
- Inactive: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E0D2`, text `#173F46`.
- Active: Background `#D7EEF0`, border `1.5px solid #0D7E8F`, text `#086574` SemiBold.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Touch target: Minimum 48×48px boundary with an internal visual box/radio of 26×26px.
- Checked State: Solid `#0D7E8F` fill with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` checkmark (stroke width 3px).
- Border: 2px solid `#173F46` when unselected to ensure stark contrast against the cream canvas.

### Clinician Notes & Provenance Badges (Anti-AI Guardrails)
- All automated assessments or tele-consult summaries must reside within a `#FFF8EC` warm card with a visible top banner stating:
  - "註冊護士審核 Registered Nurse Verified" or "註冊營養師建議 Clinical Dietitian Advice".
  - Reviewer license code and review timestamp displayed in 15px `#5C7477`.
  - Standardized disclaimer: Clear, non-technical plain text explaining that data is auxiliary to clinical doctor appointments.
