Build a simple bilingual SEO blog website for Glukky in English and Traditional Chinese. The site should support organic traffic, educate users, and drive App Store downloads. The setup must stay low-cost, simple, and maintainable, with no unnecessary engineering. Use subdirectories for language structure because this is the simplest and most SEO-friendly approach for a bilingual site on one domain.

Language and URL structure
Use this exact structure:

/ → redirect or serve English homepage

/blog

/blog/[english-slug]

/about

/app

Traditional Chinese:

/zh/

/zh/blog/

/zh/blog/[english-or-clean-romanized-slug]

/zh/about/

/zh/app/

Rules:

Keep the Chinese content inside /zh/ only.

Do not mix Chinese and English articles in the same folder.

Each English article should have a corresponding Traditional Chinese version where applicable.

Cross-link equivalent language versions clearly on each page.

Use hreflang and canonical tags correctly for EN and ZH pages.

Keep URL slugs short and stable. English slugs are acceptable for Chinese pages if that is simpler to maintain. This is often easier and less error-prone than Chinese-character URLs.
