Brief Structure
1. The Weekly Planner (Focus)
Every week, the system assigns exactly two goals:
One Physical Goal: Usually a short post-dinner "Night Walk" to clear glucose spikes.
One Diet or Timing Goal: Focused on when you eat or how you eat (e.g., managing late dinners or specific food struggles).
2. The Negotiation & Escalation Engine
The planner grows with you through a specific hierarchy:
Frequency First: We prioritize moving more often (days per week) before moving longer (minutes per walk).
De-escalation: If you have a rough couple of weeks, the planner automatically shrinks your goals to a "Standing Reset" (just 2 minutes) to keep the habit alive.
Auto-Escalation: Once you master the small steps, the planner gently "nudges" you to the next level.
3. The Sorted Diet Queue
Instead of fixing everything at once, your dietary struggles (like Eating Out, Sugary Drinks, or Snacking) are placed in a priority queue. You only move to the next struggle once you’ve achieved 90% consistency over 3 weeks, ensuring the habit is truly "mastered."

The Mitigation Trio Rotation Rules
One at a Time: I will only suggest one strategy (Fiber Starter, Dusk Prep, or Split Dinner) per week.
The "Success" Graduation: If a user achieves 100% success with a tip for 3 consecutive weeks, I will move to the next tip to build their "toolkit."
The "Failure" Pivot: If a user is unable to do it at all (0% success) for one week, I will pivot to a different tip the following week to find a better fit for their lifestyle.

🚶‍♂️ Movement: Solving the "Glycemic Gap"
The logic now prevents the user from becoming a "Weekend Warrior" at the expense of daily health:
• The Duration Cap: Once all scheduled walks hit 20 minutes, the system stops asking for more time.
• The Education Pivot: I will explain that a 40-minute walk on Monday cannot "clear" the glucose spike from a sedentary Tuesday dinner.
• The Standing Bridge: If they cannot add a full walk day, we introduce the Standing Reset (2 min) as the lowest-friction way to break the sedentary pattern on their "off" days.

🥗 The Revised Diet Progression Logic
we use a Weekly Gatekeeper system:
The Goal: Follow the specific tip (e.g., "1:1 Juice Dilution") every time the situation arises.
The "No" Reset: If a user hits even one "No" during the week, they stay on that same tip next week.
The Graduation: If the user hits only "Yes" or "No Chance" for the entire week (7/7), they move to the next tip in that struggle's ladder.
Struggle Mastery: Once the user has successfully cleared all tips in a struggle's ladder (e.g., all 3 Sugary Drink tips), they move to the next major struggle in the priority queue (e.g., Snacks).

Weekly Feedback & Planning
This happens every 7 days to set the goals for the upcoming week.
1. Physical Negotiation (Movement)
• The Check: Did the user hit their scheduled frequency (days/week)?
• The Pivot: * If Frequency < 5/7: Ask for +1 day.
    ◦ If "No" to +1 day: Ask for +5 min to one existing walk (Max 20 min).
• The Glycemic Gap: If all walks are 20 min and user still won't add days: Explain the "Gap" and suggest a Standing Reset (2 min) for a non-walk day.
• The Timing Pivot: If "Move Dinner Earlier" (Level 1) had 0% success: Switch to the Fiber Starter (Level 2).
2. Diet Tip Ladder (The "Clean Week" Rule)
• The Check: Did the user have any "No" responses for the weekly tip?
• The Pivot:
    ◦ Success (All "Yes" or "No Chance"): Move to the next specific tip in that struggle's ladder.
    ◦ Failure (Any "No"): Stay on the same tip for the next week.
🗓️ Bi-Weekly Feedback (Every 2 Weeks)
This is the "Habit Bridge" check to prevent stagnation or total abandonment.
• The Walking Bridge: If 0 total walks for 2 consecutive weeks $\rightarrow$ De-escalate to Standing Reset (2 min).
• The Auto-Escalation: If 100% success on Standing Reset for 2 consecutive weeks $\rightarrow$ Mandatory upgrade to a 5-minute walk.
• Stagnation Check: If the user agreed to an extra day but failed for 2 weeks $\rightarrow$ Stop asking for frequency; pivot back to asking for duration instead.
🗓️ Monthly Feedback & Learning (Every 3-4 Weeks)
This is the "Deep Dive" into patterns and mastery graduation.
1. Fatigue Pattern Learning (3-Week Cycle)
• The Check: Analyze "Tired" logs from the daily check-ins.
• The Action: If the same day of the week was marked "Tired" 3 out of 3 times $\rightarrow$ Propose one permanent Rest Day (Max 1/week).
2. Diet Struggle Graduation
• The Check: Has the user successfully cleared all tips in the current struggle's ladder?
• The Action: Move to the next major struggle in the priority queue (Eat Out → Sugary Food/Drink → Snacks → Portion Control).
3. Monthly Report
• The Analysis: Summarize which diet tips had the most "No" vs. "Yes" vs. "No Chance" responses to identify the user's biggest hurdles.
• Physical Tank: Review total minutes moved over the month to show progress in volume.
