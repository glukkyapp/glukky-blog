Glucose Threshold System
What we are building
Two phases that classify a meal's glucose impact using the user's actual post-meal reading (HStix) for those who log. Phase 1 uses fixed clinical thresholds based on the user's condition. Phase 2 personalises those thresholds once enough readings are collected. Both phases always cap at the universal clinical ceiling.

Phase 1 — Fixed thresholds

Once the user's group is known, classify every post-meal reading using these values:
Healthy / Pre-DM group
Low: 5.9 mmol/L or below
Medium: 6.0 to 7.8 mmol/L
High: above 7.8 mmol/L

T2DM group
Low: 7.5 mmol/L or below
Medium: 7.6 to 9.9 mmol/L
High: 10.0 mmol/L or above

Apply these thresholds everywhere a meal impact colour or label is shown — the home page weekly summary (chart 1), the daily report/todays meal dotstrip (chart 2), food ranking with colors (chart 4/5), weekly grid (chart 6) the monthly donut (chart 7).

Phase 2 — Personalised thresholds
Once a user has recorded 15 or more post-meal readings, the app switches to personalised thresholds automatically.
The personalised thresholds are calculated from the user's own reading history:
The medium/low boundary is set at the point where 75% of their readings fall below it
The high boundary is set at the point where 90% of their readings fall below it — but it can never exceed the Phase 1 "high" threshold for their group. e.g if ones 90% is 8.1, but the universal definition for high is 7.8, today they log a meal hstix and its 7.9, it is still showing as high, not medium. The clinical ceiling is always enforced.
These thresholds are recalculated nightly. No user action is needed.
