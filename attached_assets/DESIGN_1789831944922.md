## Brand & Style
This design system provides a dignified, warm, and hyper-legible interface tailored for older adults in Hong Kong managing their daily health, medications, vitals, and dietary habits. The emotional tone avoids the sterile, anxiety-inducing aesthetics of clinical software; instead, it projects steady reliability, warmth, and vitality.

The visual style blends high-contrast functional accessibility with soft, physical tactile cues. Every interactive element maintains physical distinctiveness through defined borders, generous proportions, and reassuring touch feedback. Visual hierarchy is direct and unambiguous, minimizing cognitive load while honoring cultural familiarity for Traditional Chinese readers in Hong Kong.

## Colors
The color palette pairs natural vitality with clinical legibility, deliberately engineered to meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AAA contrast ratios across key reading states.

- **Primary (`#0D5C3A` - Jade Emerald):** Conveys vital health, calm endurance, and balance. Used for active health indicators, medication confirmations, and stable readings.
- **Secondary (`#D95D0F` - Roasted Tangerine):** A high-visibility, warm culinary orange. Dedicated to urgent nudges, evening meal reminders, and primary calls-to-action such as dinner suggestions and hydration checks.
- **Tertiary (`#1E3A5F` - Deep Harbor Slate):** Supplies structure and authority. Replaces pure black for headings, primary text, and structural icons, delivering sharp contrast without the glare of harsh black on pure white.
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F4` - Warm Rice Paper):** A soft, eye-resting background inspired by warm textured paper, cutting blue-light glare and optical fatigue commonly experienced by aging eyes.

### Functional Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#FBF9F4`
- **Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Muted Text:** `#465A6E` (guarantees a minimum 5.5:1 ratio against card white)
- **High-Alert / Warning:** `#B91C1C`
- **Dividers & Accessible Borders:** `#D6D0C4`

## Typography
Typography is prioritized for maximum legibility under presbyopia, cataract haze, and mild visual impairments. The system combines **Noto Sans** (optimized for Traditional Chinese character stroke clarity) with **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** for Western numerals, medication dosages, times, and biometric readings.

### Character Density and Traditional Chinese Adaptation
- Strict minimum font threshold: no functional text under `16px`.
- Line heights maintain a minimum factor of `1.5x` to `1.6x` body size, preventing complex multi-stroke Traditional Chinese glyphs (e.g., 藥, 體, 護) from bleeding into neighboring lines.
- Dynamic Type and OS-level Font Scaling (`Aa` scale) support: all containers must flex vertically. Never fix container heights with static pixel cutoffs.
- Avoid ultra-thin or light font weights entirely; start text at weight `400` or `500` and headings at `600` or `700`.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a resilient single-column flow on mobile viewports and a restrained 2-to-4 column grid on tablets and desktop monitors. The goal is predictable vertical scanning, avoiding complex nested grids or multi-pane split views that introduce disorientation.

### Touch Target Standard
- Every interactive element mandates a strict minimum touch area of `48px × 48px`, ideally `56px` for critical navigation actions.
- Spacing between adjacent interactive controls must not be less than `12px` (`space-sm`) to completely prevent mis-taps caused by hand tremors or imprecise finger placement.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Rules
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column stack, full-width cards, fixed bottom navigation bar with prominent labels beneath large icons.
- **Tablet & Large Screens (≥ 640px):** Max container width capped at `720px` to maintain comfortable, narrow eye-sweep ranges during reading. Content centers cleanly on the neutral backdrop.

## Elevation & Depth
Visual hierarchy avoids flat ambiguity and ethereal glassmorphism. Depth is created through tangible, grounded planes inspired by tactile paper and physical switches.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Backdrop):** Solid Warm Rice Paper (`#FBF9F4`) with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Static Containers):** Surface pure white (`#FFFFFF`), bordered by a distinct 1.5px outline (`#D6D0C4`) and supported by a soft, warm ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -1px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Buttons & Active States):** Enhanced physical separation: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.14)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Emergency Confirmations):** Anchored elevated dialogs: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.22)`, accompanied by a high-contrast dimming scrim (`rgba(14, 28, 44, 0.65)`).

## Shapes
A roundedness value of `2` provides friendly, welcoming boundaries while maintaining strong geometric definition.

- Standard buttons and cards feature `16px` (`rounded-lg`) corner radii.
- Secondary badges, numeric tag bubbles, and alert pills use `24px` (`rounded-xl`).
- High-contrast 1.5px to 2px continuous outer borders reinforce boundaries, ensuring elements remain clear for users with low contrast sensitivity or compromised peripheral vision.

## Components

### Action Buttons
- **Primary Health Actions (Confirm Meds, Save Log):** Solid Emerald Green (`#0D5C3A`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), min-height `56px`, corner radius `16px`. Active press state deepens background to `#083B25` with a subtle inner press shadow.
- **Secondary Culinary / Dinner CTA:** Solid Warm Tangerine (`#D95D0F`) background, bold white text, min-height `56px`. Paired with an easily recognizable food icon (e.g., rice bowl or steam cloche).
- **Tertiary / Dismiss Actions:** White background, 2px solid Harbor Slate border (`#1E3A5F`), Harbor Slate text.

### Cards & Information Panels
- Pure white background framed with a 1.5px `#D6D0C4` border and Level 1 elevation.
- Generous internal padding (`space-md` or `space-lg`).
- High-contrast category tags positioned at the top left in traditional Chinese characters, using bold font weights.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Large min-height `56px` with an inner background of `#FFFFFF`.
- 2px outer border in `#465A6E`; borders transition to 3px solid Emerald Green (`#0D5C3A`) on focus with an accompanying outer ring.
- Placeholder text is avoided for critical instructions; use persistent, clear labels above the field with explicit format hints (e.g., 「請輸入收縮壓，例如 120」).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Large physical bounds: minimum `28px × 28px` box size inside a `56px` tap-friendly row container.
- Active states feature bold, heavy checkmarks (3px stroke) in primary green.
- The entire row is clickable, featuring a light green highlight background (`#E7F2EC`) upon selection to give instantaneous feedback.

### Lists & Navigation
- List items must be separated by visible 1px borders (`#E5E0D5`) rather than relying purely on negative space.
- Right-aligned directional chevrons (`>`) must be thickened to 2.5px to indicate tappability clearly.
- Bottom navigation features large iconography accompanied by permanent, non-collapsing text labels in Traditional Chinese (e.g., 「首頁」, 「用藥」, 「飲食建議」, 「健康記錄」).

### Quick-Adjustment Utility (Aa Sizer)
- A persistent or easily accessible text-scaling widget anchored at the top-right of primary screens, allowing immediate toggle between standard (`100%`), large (`120%`), and extra-large (`140%`) typographic scales without leaving the app context.
