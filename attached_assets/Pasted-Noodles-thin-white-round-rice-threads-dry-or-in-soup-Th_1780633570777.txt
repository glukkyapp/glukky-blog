Noodles
米粉 — thin, white, round rice threads, dry or in soup. Thinner than 米線, straight.

米線 — white, round, slightly thicker than 米粉, smooth surface, always in soup.

河粉 — wide, flat, opaque white strips, silky surface, often stir-fried or in soup.

幼麵 — thin yellow egg noodles, wiry and springy, always in soup or tossed.

粗麵 — thick yellow egg noodles, chewy, wider than 幼麵.

公仔麵 — yellow, wavy/crimped instant noodles.

腸粉 — rolled rice noodle sheets, soft, shiny, tube-like.

米麵 — avoid if unsure; use the specific noodle type above.

Rice and Congee
白飯 — plain white, loose steamed grains, bright white colour.

紅米飯 — reddish-brown rice, visibly darker than white rice.

白粥 — plain pale congee, smooth and watery.

皮蛋瘦肉粥 — congee with visible dark translucent egg pieces and pork shreds.

Cha Chaan Teng Drinks
凍檸茶 — cold amber tea in glass with ice, lemon slice visible on rim or inside.

熱檸茶 — same amber tea, served hot in ceramic cup or glass, lemon slice, no ice.

凍奶茶 — cold milky brown tea in glass with ice, no lemon, opaque from milk.

熱奶茶 — hot milky brown tea, often in ceramic tea cup.

好立克 — milk white, creamy drink.

阿華田 — similar to milk tea but more reddish-brown; milk tea is more yellowish-brown. If you see a ceramic tea cup with sugar cube or packed sugar next to the cup, make it hot milk tea. A drink without sugar cube or packed sugar does not mean it is not milk tea. If uncertain between 奶茶 and 阿華田, return both choices and let the user pick in a pop-up.

Rule for hot milk tea: if a ceramic tea cup appears with sugar cube or packed sugar, make it 熱奶茶.

Cha Chaan Teng Food
炒滑蛋 — soft pale-yellow scrambled egg, glossy, no browning. NOT salmon.

煎蛋 — fried egg with set white and visible yolk, edges may be crispy.

奶油豬 — thick white bun/bread with butter and condensed milk.

蒜蓉包 — bun with visible garlic topping, golden-brown surface.

多士 — thin and toasted only.

西多士 — deep-fried French toast, golden-brown and thick, served with butter and syrup.

Meat
牛扒 — thick slab or sliced thin of beef, clear grill marks or seared brown surface, served on plate or on noodles

叉燒 — reddish-brown glazed pork, sliced or in chunks, caramelised shiny surface. Never a whole slab.

豬潤 — dark sliced liver in soup, smoother and less meaty-looking than beef.

牛肉 — thinner beef slices, less dense than 牛扒.

牛丸 — darker, slightly textured beef ball.

魚蛋 — pale yellow/white, smooth fish ball.

Common Confusions
豬潤 vs 牛肉 — both dark sliced meat in soup, but 豬潤 is liver (less meaty pattern, appears smoother).

魚蛋 vs 牛丸 — both spherical in soup, but 魚蛋 is pale yellow/white and smooth, 牛丸 is darker and slightly textured.

西多士 vs 多士 — 西多士 is deep-fried French toast, golden-brown and thick, served with butter and syrup. 多士 is thin and toasted only.

公仔麵 vs 幼麵 — both thin noodles in soup but 公仔麵 is wavy/crimped, 幼麵 is straight.

炒滑蛋 vs salmon — scrambled egg is soft, pale yellow, glossy.

好立克 vs 阿華田 vs 熱奶茶 — use cup color and sugar cues; tea cup + sugar points to 熱奶茶.

Good Extra Rules to Add
If a dish could be one of two items and the image is ambiguous, return both choices and let the user choose.

If not sure, do not assume.

For drinks, prefer container + colour + garnish cues over guessing by shade alone.

Keep descriptions short so the prompt does not overload the model.
