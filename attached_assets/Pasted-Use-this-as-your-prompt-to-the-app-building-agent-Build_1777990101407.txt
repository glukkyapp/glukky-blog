Use this as your prompt to the app-building agent.

Build a simple SEO blog website for Glukky to support organic traffic and App Store discovery. The site should target people in Hong Kong / Traditional Chinese and English users who are trying to manage or prevent diabetes through food habits, post-meal walking, dinner timing, and lower-GI eating. The blog's job is to rank for search queries, educate users, and funnel them to download Glukky. The setup must stay low-cost, simple, and maintainable. Use a static site or very lightweight setup only; avoid complex CMS, database, auth, or server infrastructure.
