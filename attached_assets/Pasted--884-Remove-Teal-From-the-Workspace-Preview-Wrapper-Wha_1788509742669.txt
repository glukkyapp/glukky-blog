#884 - Remove Teal From the Workspace Preview Wrapper
What & Why
The direct mockup preview is already warm cream across its entire document and wide viewport. The remaining teal is outside the application document, in the workspace/Replit embedding or presentation layer. Fix that outer layer so the workspace presentation matches the app instead of changing the app screen again.

Done looks like
The affected mockups remain unchanged inside their iframe documents
In the actual Replit workspace presentation, the entire user-visible preview area outside and around each portrait iframe is #FEF2E0
No teal, blue, white, transparent, or fallback-colored gutters remain in the parent presentation surface
The fix survives a workspace reload and reopening the canvas/preview
The iframe content and the parent presentation surface meet without a visible color boundary
Direct preview routes continue to render exactly as they do now
Out of scope
Changing html, body, #root, preview-wrapper, or mockup-component backgrounds inside the application iframe
Recoloring semantic teal/blue elements within the mockup UI
Changing mockup layout, copy, dimensions, or interactions
Implementing the separate visual regression task
Steps
Inspect outside the iframe — In the actual Replit workspace presentation, inspect the iframe element, its parent and ancestor containers, canvas/preview host surface, and any Replit-specific frame configuration. Determine which first painted ancestor outside the iframe produces the visible teal.
Confirm the boundary — Verify the iframe document itself remains #FEF2E0 at its edges while the sampled teal belongs to a parent/host surface outside that document.
Fix the source layer — Change the real presentation or frame configuration that paints the teal. Do not cover it with another application-level element and do not modify the already-correct mockup CSS.
Persist the presentation fix — Store the corrected Replit/canvas frame configuration in the durable source used to reconstruct the workspace presentation, not only in transient live canvas state.
Verify in the workspace — Reload or reopen the actual Replit workspace canvas/preview and check every affected frame at both portrait and wide workspace sizes. Capture evidence that includes space outside the iframe, not merely a screenshot of the direct preview URL.
Relevant areas
Replit workspace preview host document and iframe ancestors outside the application document
Canvas/preview wrapper configuration for the affected iframe shapes
Durable Replit-specific frame state under .canvas/, if that state is the source used to reconstruct the presentation
